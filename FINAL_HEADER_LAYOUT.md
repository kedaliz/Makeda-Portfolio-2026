## Visual Hierarchy

1. **Navigation Bar** (top)
   - Subtle, doesn't distract
   - Just functional links

2. **Your Name** (Makeda Elizabeth Cabey)
   - **FIRST THING** that really stands out
   - Beautiful gradient colors
   - Large and bold (2.8rem)

3. **Your Story** (Bio paragraph)
   - Coral red color
   - Tells your authentic story
   - About 1.1rem

4. **Your Passion** (Quote)
   - Coral red in peach box
   - Italicized for emphasis
   - Your values highlighted

5. **Projects** (Below)
   - Your work and accomplishments

## Why This Works

✅ **Super Clean Start** — Just navigation at top, doesn't clutter the view
✅ **Your Name First** — "Makeda Elizabeth Cabey" is what users see first
✅ **Warm Colors** — Coral red text feels personal and inviting
✅ **Light Background** — Nothing looks "heavy" or formal
✅ **Clear Hierarchy** — Navigation → Name → Story → Work
✅ **Professional** — Minimal design approach
✅ **Unique** — Your warm color palette makes it yours
